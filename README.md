# Saint's Medical Intervention

A mod for Fallout: New Vegas / Tale of Two Wastelands expanding treatment of limb injuries in the field, giving vanilla medical items a use and more weight to the Medicine skill and doctor services. Inspired by the Fallout 3 mod "Triage", originally developed by Kearsage, and the spirit of Fallout Wanderer's Edition. Written in NVSE, ESPLess.

## Mechanics

Using a Doctor's Bag will open a menu providing you with options.
